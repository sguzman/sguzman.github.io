## Ambition

Create a premium, local-first Kanban experience that combines the performance of a native Rust backend with the flexibility of a modern WASM frontend.

## What’s novel

- Hybrid Architecture: Leverages Tauri and Yew for a seamless desktop experience with SQLite persistence.
- Gapped Positioning: Implements a sophisticated 'gapped-position' strategy for efficient card reordering without full-table rewrites.
- End-to-End Tracing: Deeply instrumented with tracing and tracing-wasm for unified diagnostic visibility across the bridge.

## Highlights
