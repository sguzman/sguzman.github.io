# MathematicaMathFun

A research-oriented repository of Wolfram Language notebooks exploring advanced topics in pure and applied mathematics.

## Overview

This repository serves as a personal computational laboratory for investigating complex mathematical structures. It contains a collection of research-grade `.nb` files focused on symbolic analysis, topological visualization, and system modeling.

## Featured Research

### 1. Algebraic Topology & Differential Geometry
- **Curvature Analysis**: Notebooks for visualizing Riemann tensors and Gaussian curvature on multi-dimensional manifolds.
- **Complex Function Theory**: Symbolic exploration of holomorphicity and conformal mapping.

### 2. Calculus of Variations & Optimization
- **Functional Minimization**: Implementations of Euler-Lagrange equations for physical systems and shortest-path problems.
- **Probability Density Models**: High-fidelity visualizations of sector-based statistical distributions.

### 3. Dynamic Systems & Fractals
- **System Stability**: Analysis of attractor basins and phase-space trajectories for non-linear oscillators.
- **Generative Geometry**: Scripts for rendering complex recursive patterns and fractal sets.

## Technical Details

- **Language**: Wolfram Language (Mathematica)
- **Primary Focus**: Symbolic computation, numerical analysis, and research-grade visualization.

## Usage

These notebooks are best viewed in **Wolfram Mathematica** or **Wolfram Desktop**. For a lightweight preview, you can use the **Wolfram Player**.
