## Overview

Symfose is a Rust desktop app for playing virtual instruments from your keyboard, starting with piano.

## Ambition

I’m building this to become a sharp, reusable tool that I can rely on in real workflows: fast, well-scoped, and easy to operate.

## What’s novel

- Opinionated defaults with room for power-user control.
- Tight scope + strong ergonomics (the “small tool, big leverage” approach).

## Highlights

- real-time instrument play from configurable keyboard mappings
- playable song charts with tempo-aware timing
- score mode (accuracy + streak + grading)
- timed/challenge modes
