## Overview

vessel is a Rust-native media extraction, download, and metadata-ledger engine that aims for yt-dlp-grade functionality while adding durable, idempotent dataset maintenance for channels, videos, playlists, comments, subtitles, and historical metadata snapshots.

## Ambition

I’m building this to become a sharp, reusable tool that I can rely on in real workflows: fast, well-scoped, and easy to operate.

## What’s novel

- Opinionated defaults with room for power-user control.
- Tight scope + strong ergonomics (the “small tool, big leverage” approach).
